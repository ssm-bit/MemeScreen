Privacy policy

MemeScreen is a student project for paper trading. It stores the email address, nickname and bcrypt-hashed password you sign up with, your paper-trading accounts (simulated cash, positions, orders and trade history), your watchlist, alert rules, saved screens and leaderboard scores. This data is kept in Back4App (Parse) and cached in your browser's local storage so the app opens quickly and works offline. Nothing else is collected: no location, no contacts, no advertising identifiers, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no cookies beyond the session that keeps you signed in. Market prices, charts and news are fetched from DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, PumpPortal, RugCheck, CoinGecko, Alternative.me and public news feeds; those requests carry no account information. We do not sell or share personal data. Email is used only to sign in.

Terms of service

MemeScreen is a simulator. Every balance, order and fill is fictional. A connected Phantom wallet is read-only: MemeScreen shows its holdings and never asks it to sign anything, so it never moves real money. Prices, grades, screens and alerts are informational, can be wrong or delayed, and are not financial advice. You must be 18 or older to create an account. Do not use the service for anything unlawful or attempt to disrupt it. The service is provided as is, without warranty, and may change or stop at any time. Leaderboard nicknames are visible to other users.

Refunds

MemeScreen is free. There is nothing to purchase and nothing to refund.

Cookies

No advertising or third-party cookies. The only stored items are your sign-in session and app settings in browser storage, which you control through "Keep me signed in" and by logging out.

Accessibility

Text and controls meet a minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio on the dark theme. Every control can be reached with a keyboard (Tab, Enter, Space, arrow keys on tables, Escape closes menus). Icons carry text labels or ARIA labels. Motion is reduced when your device asks for it. Tap targets on phones are at least 44 points.

Delete your data

You can delete your account and everything tied to it at any time. Deletion removes your user record, paper accounts, profile and leaderboard rows from Back4App and clears this browser's cache. It cannot be undone.
