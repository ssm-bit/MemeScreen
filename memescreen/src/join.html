MemeScreen on your phone

Any number of phones can join at once. Everyone gets their own login and their own paper accounts.

Expo Go

iPhone and Android, with real phone notifications
Tap the Expo account you are signed in with:
  1. Install Expo Go from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Sign in to Expo Go, then tap your Expo account name above this code. Each account has its own code.
  3. Join the same Wi-Fi or hotspot as the presenter's laptop.
  4. iPhone: scan with the Camera. Android: scan inside Expo Go.
  5. Tap Create account and start trading.

No install

  1. Join the same Wi-Fi or hotspot as the presenter's laptop.
  2. Scan with the phone camera and open the link.
